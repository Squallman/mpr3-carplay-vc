multiple cluster transport endpoints; it does not prove which is active in
P3695 vehicle configuration.

  "Usage: versedt set_displayable DISPLAYID DISPLAYABLE"
  generated IVideoEncoding proxy types and
  call_struct_DisplayID_I32IVideoEncodingRequest.
This proves a client-side proxy shape, but no native-navigation call site with
a constant displayable was proven.

videoencoderservice is proven to consume an integer displayable ID through
IpTeConnection::getDisplayable, dimension validation, encoder creation, and
CEncoder::feed(IpteDisplayable). No whitelist/owner check was proven.
