P3695 video encoding client search

TFFS signature hits included videoencoderservice itself and diagnostic/sample
clients (encoderal_spec, encoding_poc, vc-ldt, mcp_viwibridge, ipte_sample,
showimage). These hits prove that the APIs are present in the image, but no
client call site was recovered that supplies the normal Audi navigation
setActiveDisplayable arguments.

videoencoderservice exports/contains the methods setActiveDisplayable,
setDisplayable, getDisplayable, requestVideoConnection and
releaseVideoConnection. Phase 2 recovered the IDL shape
comm_idl_calls::call_struct_DisplayID_I32IVideoEncodingReply and a plausible
displayID plus I32 displayable signature. The exact method-number mapping and
normal client remain UNKNOWN.

The native navigation executable navStartup directly imports display-init APIs,
not the video encoding service. The HMI JavaScript has display-manager lookup
calls but no setActiveDisplayable literal. Therefore no proven stock caller is
reported here.

Required next evidence is a generated proxy or service-discovery trace tying a
specific client to method 9/10 (or another method), with constant display ID
and displayable ID arguments. This phase did not obtain that proof.
