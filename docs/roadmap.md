# Roadmap

## Stage 0 — completed

Static firmware extraction and architecture reverse engineering.

## Stage 1 — completed

Offline mocked prototype preserving stock stream 110.

## Stage 2 — next

Improve offline contracts, recover the advertisement descriptor/callback, and
resolve the P3695 iAP2 capability gate.

## Stage 3

Target cross-build only; validate exports and linking without vehicle deployment.

## Stage 4

Authorized bench/runtime diagnostics: loader policy, COMM policy, displayable
occupancy, and cluster endpoint detection.

## Stage 5

Controlled vehicle proof of concept only after the preceding contracts are
proven and separately authorized.
